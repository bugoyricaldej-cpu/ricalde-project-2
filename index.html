A Legacy of Faith and Education

Founded in 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College has served the community of Kabankalan City through Catholic education. KCC aims to form learners with strong academics, faith, character, and a heart for service.

School motto: “Teach Ye All Nations” — Matthew 28:19.
